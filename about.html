ShubhDin — शुभ दिन
Your Daily Hindu Devotional Companion

Shubh Din is a Vedic astrology and devotional app for Hindu households. It is run from Eluru, Andhra Pradesh.

What the app does

📜 Free Kundli Report

A full birth chart report — 17 chapters, over 250 pages — generated from your date, time and place of birth. Ashtakavarga house strength, yogas, doshas, the Vimshottari dasha down to pratyantar level, transits, numerology and remedies. It is free, and it is the same calculation the paid reports build on.

📿 Daily Panchang

Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and vara for your own city, with sunrise, sunset, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika, Abhijit, Amrit Kaal, Brahma Muhurta and the full sixteen choghadiya. Everything is computed on your device by our own engine using standard astronomical algorithms and the Lahiri ayanamsa — the same basis a printed panchang uses. Nothing is fetched from an outside astrology service.

⏰ Shubh and Ashubh Times

Recommended windows are not simply the auspicious choghadiya. Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika, Varjyam and Dur Muhurtam are cut out of them first, so the app never recommends and forbids the same minutes. That is why some windows appear shortened, and why on some days there is honestly none left.

🕉️ Mantras and Jap

A daily deity mantra with a tap-counted jap mala, chant audio, and the words of the mantra highlighting in time with the recitation so you can follow along. Your streak carries across days. A sadhana can also be started for a particular concern — marriage, money, health or study. You pick the concern, and the app sets a mantra, a daily count and a length of days, usually twenty-one. It keeps its own day-by-day streak, separate from the daily jap.

🏠 Muhurta Finder

Auspicious dates for griha pravesh, starting a business or buying a vehicle, scored against your own birth chart — your tarabala and chandrabala, not a generic list. Free to use.

💑 Marriage Compatibility

Ashtakoota guna milan from two birth charts — all eight kootas shown separately, the Mangal Dosha verdict, and whether Nadi, Bhakoot and Gana dosha stand or are cancelled by classical parihara. Free to use.

🗓️ Hindu Calendar

Vrats, festivals, samvat, masa, ritu and ayana — computed, not listed from a table, so the dates follow your city's sunrise.

💎 Paid Reports

Alongside everything above, six detailed reports can be purchased: Marriage Compatibility, Love and Relationship, Career and Wealth, Child and Family, Annual Varshaphal, and the 10-Year Forecast. Prices are listed on the Pricing page. Each is a one-time purchase delivered as a PDF in the app.

Nine languages

English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Gujarati, Bengali, Marathi and Assamese — reviewed by native speakers rather than machine-translated.

How we work

Every figure in the app is calculated from your birth details. Where a classical text is ambiguous we say so rather than picking a side quietly, and where a day has no auspicious window we say that too instead of manufacturing one. An astrological reading is guidance for your own reflection — for anything that matters, take the final call with your family purohit.
