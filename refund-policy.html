Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Reports on ShubhDin are digital documents, prepared from the birth details you enter and delivered immediately. This page says when we refund and when we cannot.

1. What you are buying

These are intangible digital goods. Nothing physical is sold, posted or shipped, so no shipping or delivery charges apply and there is no delivery address to give. Delivery is instant and electronic: the report appears in the app as soon as it is generated.

Each paid report is generated for your own birth details at the moment you buy it. There is no physical delivery and nothing is shipped. Your report appears under My Reports in the app and can be downloaded as a PDF.

2. If the report never arrived

If your payment succeeded but no report appeared, write to us from the Contact page with the payment reference. We will either deliver the report or refund you in full.

3. If you were charged twice

Duplicate payments for the same report are refunded in full. Tell us the payment reference and we will return the extra amount.

4. If the birth details were wrong

A report is only as correct as the details it was made from. If you entered a wrong date, time or place, write to us — we will regenerate the report with the corrected details at no extra cost. This is usually more useful than a refund, since the corrected report is the one you actually wanted.

5. When we cannot refund

Once a report has been correctly generated and delivered from the details you entered, we cannot refund it — the work is done and the document is already in your hands. We also cannot refund on the basis that you disagree with what the report says: astrology is a matter of tradition and interpretation, and the calculations are shown openly in the report so you can check them yourself.

6. How long we take to decide

We reply to every refund request within 3 working days. If we need the payment reference or your registered email to find the transaction, we will ask in that first reply.

7. How long the money takes to arrive

Approved refunds are sent back through Razorpay to the same method you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it.

8. Cancelling

There is no subscription and nothing recurring. Each report is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel.

9. Reaching us

For anything on this page, write from the Contact page. Please include the payment reference so we can find the transaction quickly.
