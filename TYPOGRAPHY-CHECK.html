Typography check — is it the render, or the extractor?

How to use this. Print this page to PDF exactly as you print a report (the same browser, the same Save-as-PDF path). Then look at the printed page with your eyes.

If the words look correct on the printed page — whole, no gaps inside a word, conjuncts joined — then the spacing you saw in the extracted text was inserted by the PDF text extractor, and there is nothing to fix. Indic shaping repositions marks, and extractors commonly emit them separately.

If the words visually break on the printed page — লগ্ন rendering as ল গ্ন, మ్యా splitting — that is a real release blocker and the cause is font loading or font substitution, not the translation.
