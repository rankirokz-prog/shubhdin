Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ShubhDin is a devotional app. This page says plainly what we collect, where it goes, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, write to us from the Contact page.

1. What we collect

To calculate a panchang or a kundli, we need the details a purohit would ask for:

  • Name — to address you, and to print on your report
  • Date, time and place of birth — these are what the calculation is actually made from
  • Your city — sunrise and all timings differ by city
  • Language — Hindi, English or one of the Indian languages you pick
  • Email — only if you sign in with Google, so your reports can be restored on another phone

For a marriage match you enter a second person's birth details. Treat those as theirs — only enter them if that person is happy for you to.

2. Where it is kept

Most of it stays on your phone, in your browser's own storage. Nothing leaves your phone until you sign in or buy a report.

If you sign in with Google, we keep your profile and your list of purchased reports on our servers (Supabase) so you can open them from any device.

3. Your report PDF

When a report is generated, the PDF is stored at a private web link tied to your account. That link is not listed anywhere and is not guessable, but anyone who has the link can open the file — so please do not post it in a group or share it publicly.

4. Who else sees your details

  • Google — only if you choose to sign in. We receive your email address; we never see your password.
  • Supabase — our hosting and database provider, where your account, purchase list and report PDFs are stored.
  • OpenStreetMap — when you search for a birth place or tap "use my current location", the place name or coordinates are sent to their free lookup service to convert it into a latitude and longitude. Your name and birth details are not sent.
  • Razorpay — when you buy a report, payment is handled by Razorpay. Your card or UPI details go to them directly and never reach us; we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded.

We do not sell your details to anyone, and we do not use advertising trackers.

5. Location

If you tap the "use my current location" button, your phone shares its coordinates so we can fill in your city. This only happens when you tap it. You can always type your city instead.

6. Removing your data

Clearing the app or browser data removes everything held on your phone. To have your account, purchase record and stored PDFs deleted from our servers, write to us from the Contact page and we will remove them.

7. How long we keep it

Your account, your list of purchases and your report PDFs stay with us until you ask us to delete them — so that a report you paid for is still there when you change phones. Anything held only on your device disappears the moment you clear the app's data. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove the stored profile and PDFs; we keep the bare record of a payment where the law requires us to.

8. Children

ShubhDin is meant for adults. A kundli may be made for a child by a parent, but the account should belong to an adult.

9. Changes

If the app changes what it collects, this page is updated with it. The date at the top tells you when it last changed.
